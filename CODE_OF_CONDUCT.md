# Code of Conduct

DevTrace is a small project and we want it to stay a respectful place to collaborate.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and constructive. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith and be patient with newcomers.
- Respect different experience levels, backgrounds and opinions.
- Keep discussions on topic and in English, so every contributor can follow them.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information, including tokens and credentials.
- Spam, trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and may block contributors who
do not follow this policy. To report a problem, contact the maintainer ([@scosme20](https://github.com/scosme20))
through GitHub. Reports are handled privately where possible.

This policy applies in the repository's issues, pull requests and any space used to discuss it.
